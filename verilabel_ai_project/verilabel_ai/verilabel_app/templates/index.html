Enterprise Architecture

Zero Tolerance for Labeling Non-Compliance

Packaging artwork discrepancies represent the #1 preventable driver of medical device recalls. VeriLabel AI enforces algorithmic rigor while preserving human authority.

Multi-Oriented Medical OCR

PaddleOCR engine optimized for high-density pharmaceutical and medical device packaging, fine-print warnings, multiline manufacturer addresses, and multilingual declarations.

ISO 15223-1 Symbol Vision

Multi-scale normalized cross-correlation detects 13 standardized medical glyphs (Sterile, Single Use, Temperature Limits, CE Mark, Notified Body ID, Manufacturer) with calibrated confidence gating.

GS1 UDI & Modulo-10 Validation

Decodes 2D DataMatrix and 1D GS1-128 carriers. Verifies Device Identifier (DI) checksum integrity and cross-matches Production Identifiers (PI Lot, PI Expiration) against label text.

17-Rule Compliance Engine

Deterministic regulatory rule engine evaluates mandatory attributes, chronological date order (MFG vs EXP), sterility claims vs visual symbols, and Rx Only declarations with severity classifications.

Qualified Expert Sign-Off

Structured Human-in-the-Loop approval workflow. Captures reviewer identity, institutional credentials, review justification, and electronic signature declaration.

21 CFR Part 11 Audit Trail

Immutable SQLite persistence logs upload cryptographic SHA-256 hashes, observed rule outcomes, and inspector electronic signatures in a permanent, auditable ledger.

Regulatory Governance

The Human-in-the-Loop Standard

Under FDA Good Machine Learning Practice (GMLP) and EU AI Act principles, AI models are classified as decision-support systems. Final commercial release authorization remains exclusively with certified human experts.

01

Artwork Ingestion

Ingests high-resolution PDF or raster artwork. CLAHE contrast preprocessing normalizes lighting and glare.

02

AI Extraction

OCR extracts textual attributes; vision algorithms detect ISO symbols; barcode decoders parse UDI carriers.

03

Rule Audit

Deterministic engine classifies discrepancies as Critical, Major, or Minor against FDA & EU MDR clauses.

04

Expert Sign-Off

Qualified regulatory officer inspects discrepancies and records electronic signature in the 21 CFR Part 11 audit ledger.
